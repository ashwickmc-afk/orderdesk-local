# Code of Conduct

OrderDesk contributors and community members are expected to make participation welcoming and constructive.

Examples of positive behaviour:
- Be respectful and patient.
- Give useful, specific feedback.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's business or customer data.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing private information or deliberately disrupting project discussions.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this code and may block contributors who repeatedly engage in harmful behaviour.
